# LowerSections Specification

- Screenshot: `docs/design-references/trionn-post-keyfacts/07-stories.png`

## Production Stories

- Pale gradient background; minimum 100vh.
- 12-column desktop grid.
- Three tab buttons at left and selected statement at right.
- Active tab changes statement, author/outcome label, thumbnail, and progress line.

## Design in Motion

- Pale gray 110vh section.
- Centered uppercase two-line title.
- Five TRIPO images arranged along an elliptical curved ribbon.
- Bottom explanatory copy and product link.

## Footer

- Near-black minimum 108vh.
- Large two-line CTA heading and real Tripo Studio link.
- Four-column information grid.
- Exact existing TRIPO logo asset is reused without modification.
